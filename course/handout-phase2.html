HANDOUT · PHASE 2 · EDUCATIONAL DEMO ONLY

When bytes escape.

This handout follows our overflow chapter, calmly and in order. We call a real strcpy into a char buffer[8]: 15 letters plus the ending zero make 16 bytes into 8. The detector reports stack-buffer-overflow and stops the program. Film 7 min 20 s, 440 s, 1920×1080.

BUFFER 8INPUT 15WRITE 16OFFSET 40
CODE · INTENTIONALLY VULNERABLE

16 bytes into 8

constexpr std::size_t BUFFER_SIZE = 8;
char buffer[BUFFER_SIZE];
strcpy(buffer, "ABCDEFGHIJKLMNO"); // 15 + null = 16 into 8, 8 past
Header says: educational demo only, intentionally vulnerable. Run only locally with -fsanitize=address,undefined.
Download .cpp Download film 8.0M
READING THE REPORT · 4 LINES

What the detector tells us

stack-buffer-overflow
You wrote past a stack buffer.
WRITE size 16
15 letters + zero.
[32,40) 'buffer' line 44
8 bytes at offset 32, declared at line 44.
at offset 40
First bad byte is 8 past the start. Then ABORTING.

Without ASan the same write may show stack smashing detected (canary) or look fine; both are allowed. That is undefined behavior.
