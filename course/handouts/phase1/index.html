Seeing the boundary.

This handout is the printable companion to our boundary chapter. We lay out one contiguous array of 20 bytes in three regions, and we draw the line we will defend later. Nothing here really overflows. Every copy is capped, so the crossing is only shown. The film runs 9 min 41 s.
