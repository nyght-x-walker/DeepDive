BREADTH 1 OF 4 · CODING STYLE AND PRECEDENCE

Operator precedence and associativity

When to add parentheses. Tap an expression to see how C++ groups it: a + b * c groups as a + (b * c), and a and b or c groups left to right. If unsure, parentheses make intent clear.

Lab on the chapter page: tap-to-group playground with style checks. Described here without widgets so this hub stays flat.

BREADTH 2 OF 4 · FLOAT AND DOUBLE

Precision: floats vs integers

Move the sliders for a and b. See why 0.1 + 0.2 leaves extra digits and why == fails on floats. Compare with integer division: 7 / 2 gives 3, the rest is dropped. Build with -Wall plus sanitizers to hear the risk early.

BREADTH 3 OF 4 · INCREMENT AND COMPOUND

i++, ++i, and operator overloading

Post vs pre: when does x get the old or the new value? Compare x = i++ against x = ++i, use += in one place, and keep overloaded ++ to step by one with expected meaning.

BREADTH 4 OF 4 · FUNCTIONS AND PATTERNS

Lambdas, structs, enums, patterns

Lambdas capture data. Higher-order functions take them as arguments. Structs hold data (struct MyData { int id; char name[10]; }, about 14 bytes, stack or heap). Factory returns objects, Singleton keeps one instance. Both reuse structs and lambdas inside.
